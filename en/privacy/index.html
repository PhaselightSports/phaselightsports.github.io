Privacy policy

Phaselight Gym Timer collects no data. The app has no account, no ads and no analytics, and sends nothing to the developer or to third parties. Your presets and settings are stored only on your device and in your own device backup; if you delete the app, they are removed from your device.

You buy the one-time unlock through Apple. The app checks the status of that purchase with Apple through the system; the developer receives no data about you.

The developer only receives crash reports if you allowed this in your device's settings (Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), through Apple and without your name.

Questions: app@phaselightsports.com

This website uses no cookies, no scripts and no analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub logs technical data such as your IP address for security purposes. See GitHub’s privacy statement.
